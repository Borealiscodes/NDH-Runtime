## 3. Canonical Guardrails (Capsule Summary)

### 🟩 **GR‑ALT — Altitude Discipline**  
All runtime operations remain within **S3–S7**.  
No governance altitude activation.  
No downward raw‑code injection.

### 🟦 **GR‑BOUND — State Bounds**  
Adjacency, resonance, flow, calm/load, and PRECL values must remain within declared envelope limits.

### 🟪 **GR‑DRIFT — Drift Neutrality**  
Solvers must not introduce untracked drift.  
Flow must integrate cleanly into calm/load.

### 🟧 **GR‑REV — Reversibility**  
Core solvers must be reversible or explicitly proven one‑way.  
No silent irreversibility.

### 🟫 **GR‑GUARD — Guardian Modulation Respect**  
Warn, Redirect, and Soften signals always constrain solver outputs.  
No solver may override guardian modulation.

### 🟥 **GR‑PRECL — PRECL Collapse Safety**  
PRECL collapse must never activate geometry or governance altitude.  
Collapse is physics‑only.
