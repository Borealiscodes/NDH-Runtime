- **Guardrail Layer**

# 2. Canonical Solvers (v2.0)

## 🟩 **Adjacency Solver (SOL‑ADJ‑v2)**  
**Role:** Update adjacency relationships under guardrail constraints.

- respects GR‑BOUND + GR‑GUARD  
- reversible under GR‑REV  
- drift‑neutral under GR‑DRIFT  
- sequenced via ORCH‑ADJ  
- feeds **SR‑ADJ** telemetry

**Expressive behavior:**  
Strengthens/weakens adjacency based on resonance + flow, never violating topology.

---

## 🟦 **Resonance Solver (SOL‑RES‑v2)**  
**Role:** Propagate, stabilize, and decay resonance.

- respects GR‑REV + GR‑GUARD  
- applies decay curves  
- sequenced via ORCH‑RES  
- feeds **SR‑RES** telemetry

**Expressive behavior:**  
Spreads resonance along adjacency paths, dampens excess into calm/load.

---

## 🟪 **Flow Solver (SOL‑FLOW‑v2)**  
**Role:** Evolve soft‑manifold flow (Ballet motion).

- respects GR‑DRIFT  
- integrates calm/load tension  
- sequenced via ORCH‑FLOW  
- feeds **SR‑FLOW** telemetry

**Expressive behavior:**  
Moves spectral regions smoothly, preserving continuity and avoiding teleportation.

---

## 🟧 **Calm/Load Solver (SOL‑CL‑v2)**  
**Role:** Integrate tension, release, and equilibrium.

- respects GR‑BOUND + GR‑ALT  
- sequenced via ORCH‑CL  
- feeds **SR‑CL** telemetry

**Expressive behavior:**  
Accumulates load from resonance/flow, releases via PRECL‑safe collapse.

---

## 🟫 **PRECL Collapse Solver (SOL‑PRECL‑v2)**  
**Role:** Safely collapse expressive geometry into runtime physics.

- respects GR‑PRECL  
- reversible where declared  
- sequenced via ORCH‑PRECL  
- feeds **SR‑PRECL** telemetry

**Expressive behavior:**  
Collapses expressive shapes into physics fields without activating governance altitude.

# 3. v2.0 Solver Invariants

- **Altitude Discipline:** S3–S7 only  
- **Guardian Alignment:** Guardian modulation always constrains outputs  
- **Invariant Preservation:** Topology, bounds, and declared invariants must hold  
- **Reversibility:** Required unless explicitly marked  
- **Drift Neutrality:** No solver may introduce untracked drift  
- **PRECL Safety:** Collapse is physics‑only, never governance  
- **Orchestration Compliance:** All solvers follow ORCH sequence
